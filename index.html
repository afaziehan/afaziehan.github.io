About

Dr. Anies Faziehan Zakaria is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Engineering Education, Faculty of Engineering and Built Environment, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), and currently serves as the department’s Head of Postgraduate Programme and Coordinator of Strategy.

Trained in technical and vocational education at Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia, with a doctoral thesis on data-driven design skill training, Dr. Anies works at the meeting point of engineering education, learning analytics and design informatics. Recent projects range from digital role-playing games for engineering ethics and drone-soccer skill development to national studies on TVET financing, competency certification and community colleges for Malaysia’s ministries.

Research

  1. Engineering education & learning analytics

    Game-based and experiential learning, engineering ethics education, educational informatics, and LLMs in programming education.

  2. Technical & vocational education and training

    Career development of TVET educators, work readiness, competency certification, and policy studies on TVET financing and rating.

  3. Design engineering & design informatics

    Data-driven product-service bundle design, pricing optimisation, customer preference modelling and multi-attribute decision-making.

  4. Statistics & machine learning

    Discrete choice and preference analysis, bibliometrics, SERVQUAL service-quality modelling and predictive models for engineering problems.

  5. STEM outreach: drones & makerspaces

    Drone-soccer skill acquisition, drone integration in schools, and sustainable makerspace activities for long-term community engagement.
